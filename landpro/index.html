What We Do

Our Services

Complete land solutions for residential, commercial and agricultural properties — one crew, one call.

Land Clearing

Removal of trees, brush and debris to open up your property for building, farming or recreation.

Forestry Mulching

Eco-friendly clearing that grinds vegetation into mulch on site — no burning, no hauling, minimal soil disturbance.

Excavation

Foundations, basements, pools, ponds and utility trenches dug with precision and care.

Grading & Leveling

Rough and finish grading for proper drainage, building pads, lawns and parking areas.

Stump Removal

Stump grinding and full root extraction so your land is truly ready to use.

Driveways & Roads

Gravel driveways, access roads and trails built to handle heavy traffic and weather.

Drainage Solutions

Ditches, culverts and French drains that keep water away from where it doesn't belong.

Demolition & Debris

Safe tear-down of small structures, sheds and old foundations, plus complete debris removal.

About LandPro

Built on Hard Work.
Driven by Results.

LandPro Excavations is a locally owned company specializing in land clearing and site preparation. We started with one machine and a simple promise: show up on time, do the job right, and leave every property better than we found it.

Today our crew and modern fleet handle projects of every size — from a single backyard lot to hundreds of acres — with the same attention to detail.

  • Honest, upfront pricing — no surprises
  • Modern, well-maintained equipment
  • Clean job sites and on-time completion
  • Fully licensed and insured for your protection
